# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Osun EduOS brings together developers, educators, public servants, parents and students. We want this project to be welcoming, respectful and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, level of experience, education, nationality or appearance.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to technology.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good intentions and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Respect privacy. Never share anyone's personal information, and never post real student, teacher or parent data.
- Focus on what is best for the schools, learners and communities the project serves.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualised language or content of any kind. This project concerns children; there is zero tolerance.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and project events, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), marking the message "Code of Conduct". Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.
