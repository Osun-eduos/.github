# Osun EduOS

**Open-source digital education management and intelligence for Osun State.**

Osun EduOS connects the Ministry of Education, education agencies, public and private schools, teachers, students and parents. It replaces manual results compilation with electronic submission and gives the Ministry reliable, current information about education across all 30 LGAs.

**Try the prototype:** https://osun-eduos.pages.dev (sample data only)
